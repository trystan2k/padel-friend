1Three directions — same screens, same content, so they compare 1:1
What changes everywhereOne primary action per screen. Status never relies on color alone — always a label or icon. All tap targets are 48px or larger. Body text is 16px, with no uppercase micro-labels. Numbers use tabular figures.
NavigationHome · Matches · Create · Community · Me. Ranking and Leagues now sit inside Community, because they're scoped to a community. The level and statistics screens move to Me. "Create match" becomes a single action you can always reach.
AccessibilityText meets WCAG AA contrast in both themes (ratios shown on the swatches). The light themes are built for sunlit courts: no low-contrast greys on tinted backgrounds. Motion is limited to a 150ms fade, and switches off under reduced-motion.
